Before you apply block · card grid

Vendor details

$30 per table

Each table gets an 8×10 ft space with weather coverage and one 6-foot table. Bring your own chairs and a covering that reaches the ground. Three tables is our maximum.

Setup & teardown

Enter from Ivanhoe at the north lot entrance starting at 8:30 am. Vehicle access closes at 10:30, and stay set up until 6:00.

Handmade & local first

Artisans, bakers, small businesses, and youth groups. Secondhand goods aren't permitted, and direct sales reps are encouraged to offer a gift basket.

Pay when you apply

Submitting takes you to secure card payment with your amount filled in. Vendors cover the small processing fee — $31.20 for one table.

The short version block · tiles

Rules at a glance

Nothing here is a surprise if you have done a market before. The full agreement is at the bottom of the application.

You bring the chairs

We supply your 6-foot table and weather coverage. You bring chairs, a covering that reaches the ground, signage, display, change, and your own bags.

No alcohol at vendor booths

Vendors may not sell alcohol or other beverages. Only a booth owned and operated by Timothy Lutheran Church may sell drinks.

Electricity is limited

Tell us your appliances, wattage, and amperage on the application — the coordinator approves every electrical request. Heaters and heated blankets aren't permitted.

Refunds are limited

Your fee is refunded only if we decline your application or products. Otherwise there are no refunds within three weeks of the market, or for a no-show.

Good to know block · FAQ

Questions vendors ask

Can I request to be next to a friend?

Yes — put it in special requests. We honor placement requests whenever the floor plan allows, and returning vendors usually keep their spot.

Is there a commission on sales?

No. The $30 table fee is all we take. What you sell is yours.

Do church groups pay the fee?

Timothy and Word of Life youth groups and ministries can apply with the fee waived — note it in special requests.

Is the market outdoors?

Yes — Weihnachtsmarkt sets up in our parking lot, and every space has weather coverage we provide.

Not a vendor?
Come shop the market, or come work it.

Free admission, free parking on Ivanhoe, and a lot full of handmade gifts and food.

About the market

A neighborhood tradition

The Timothy Christmas Market is an annual outdoor market bringing the Lindenwood Park community together each December. Local artisans and vendors set up in the church grounds — offering handmade goods, food, and festive fun for families and neighbors of all kinds.

The market is free and open to the public. It's one of our favorite ways to be a blessing to the neighborhood God has planted us in.
