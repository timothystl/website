Step by Step

Giving here isn't an obligation — it's a participation with God in what he's already doing. Here's how to set it up in a couple of minutes.

⏱ ~2 minutes

No account or login required. Every gift is processed securely by Tithe.ly and generates an automatic emailed receipt for your records.

Go to timothystl.org/give and click the Give Online button. This carries you to our secure Tithe.ly giving form — no app or account needed.
