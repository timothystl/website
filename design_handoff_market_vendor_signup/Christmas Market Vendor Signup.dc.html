Before you apply

Vendor details

$30 per table

Each space includes one 8-foot table and two chairs. Most vendors take one table; three is our maximum.

Setup & teardown

Doors open to vendors at 8:30 am. Please be set up by 10:30 and stay until 6:00 — early teardown thins out the market.

Handmade & local first

Artisans, bakers, small businesses, and youth groups. We keep duplicate product categories limited so everyone sells well.

Pay when you apply

Submitting takes you to secure card payment with your amount filled in. Vendors cover the small processing fee — $31.20 for one table.

The short version

Rules at a glance

Nothing here is a surprise if you have done a market before. The full agreement is at the bottom of the application.

You bring the booth

Table covering, signage, display, change, and your own bags. We supply the table, and the crowd.

Food vendors

Prepared and packaged food is welcome. You are responsible for meeting City of St. Louis health department requirements for what you sell.

Electricity is limited

Tell us your appliances, wattage, and amperage on the application. Bring your own extension cord and surge protector.

Table fee is non-refundable

Your space is held once payment arrives. If you have to cancel, let Marla know early so someone on the waiting list can take it.

Good to know

Questions vendors ask

Can I request to be next to a friend?

Yes — put it in special requests. We honor placement requests whenever the floor plan allows, and returning vendors usually keep their spot.

What if I don't have photos yet?

Apply anyway. Photos are optional — last year's inventory or works in progress is plenty, and you can email them to Marla later.

Is there a commission on sales?

No. The $30 table fee is all we take. What you sell is yours.

Do church groups pay the fee?

Timothy and Word of Life youth groups and ministries can apply with the fee waived — note it in special requests and Marla will sort it out.

Is the market indoors?

Tables are set up inside the church and gym, so weather is never a problem. A few food vendors work from the lot.

Can I share a table with someone?

Sure — list both names under participants and apply once. One fee covers the table however many of you are behind it.
